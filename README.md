# Excel-Sales-Data-Analysis

Project Overview
  This project explores sales data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate business performance, identify trends, and generate actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Examine sales trends and patterns.
- Highlight opportunities for business improvement.
  
Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query for data cleaning and transformation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Data visualization

Project Process
1. Reviewed and cleaned the dataset.
2. Organized and analyzed sales data.
3. Created summary tables and visualizations.
4. Identified key findings and potential business recommendations.

Key Insights
- Revenue and profitability: The business generated approximately ₦2.33 billion in revenue and ₦465.67 million in reported profit, representing an approximate profit-to-revenue ratio of 20%.
- Product performance:  Profitability varies significantly across products, with Laptop A13 contributing substantially more profit at approximately ₦105.34 million than the lowest-performing products, Office Chair Pro with profit at approximately ₦18.07 million.
- Sales representative performance: The difference between the highest- and lowest-performing representatives suggests opportunities to review sales strategies and performance across the team. Peter recorded the highest displayed sales revenue at approximately ₦484.81 million.
- Cost distribution: COGS was relatively evenly distributed across Abuja, Kano, Lagos, and Port Harcourt, with each city accounting for approximately 23%–26% of the total.
- Customer trends: Monthly customer counts varied between January and April, increasing from 520 in January to 536 in April. However, the unusually low May value requires further validation.
  
Business Recommendations
- Monitor revenue, COGS, and profit together to improve profitability and cost control.
- Investigate the factors driving strong profits for Laptop A13 and assess whether sales of lower-profit products can be improved through pricing reviews, promotions, or cost reduction.
- Compare city-level revenue, costs, and profit to identify opportunities for operational improvement.
- Strengthen customer retention through targeted promotions and customer engagement strategies.
- Identify and share effective practices from top-performing representatives, while providing targeted support to lower-performing representatives. Evaluate performance against sales targets and profit contribution before making final assessments.

Project Outcome

  This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform sales data into an interactive dashboard for monitoring business performance. The analysis highlights key performance indicators, product profitability, sales representative contributions, city-level cost distribution, and customer trends to support data-driven decision-making.
